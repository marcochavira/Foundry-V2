What is included in the price?

Physician consultation, personalized protocol, dose titration, unlimited follow-up, and discreet US shipping. Semaglutide $169/mo. Tirzepatide $239/mo. No hidden fees.

What is the difference between semaglutide and tirzepatide?

Semaglutide targets the GLP-1 receptor. Tirzepatide targets both GLP-1 and GIP — a dual mechanism that showed higher average weight reduction in trials for some patients. Your provider recommends the right one.

Are these medications FDA-approved?

Compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are not FDA-approved finished drug products. They are prepared by licensed 503B outsourcing facilities and prescribed at the clinical judgment of a licensed physician.

Where are the medications made?

US-made at 503B-certified outsourcing facilities, batch-tested above 99.9% purity. We do not source from overseas compounders.

How much weight can I expect to lose?

Clinical trials showed ~15% average reduction with semaglutide and up to 22% with tirzepatide over 68 weeks with lifestyle changes. Individual results vary.

What states do you serve?

All 50 states through our licensed telehealth network. Medication ships directly to your door.

What is NAD+ and how does it work with GLP-1?

NAD+ supports cellular energy, DNA repair, and metabolism. It's often paired with GLP-1 programs to reduce fatigue during caloric restriction. Your provider determines if it's appropriate for your protocol.

Important safety information

GLP-1 receptor agonists including compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide may have serious side effects, including possible thyroid tumors. Do not use if you or your family have a history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) or MEN 2. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved drugs. All medications prescribed at independent clinical judgment of licensed physicians. Common side effects include nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, and constipation.
