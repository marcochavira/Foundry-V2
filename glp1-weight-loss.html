What is included in the price?

Both programs include your physician consultation, personalized protocol and dose titration, unlimited follow-up, side effect management, and discreet US shipping. Semaglutide starts at $169/month. Tirzepatide starts at $239/month. No hidden fees.

What is the difference between semaglutide and tirzepatide?

Semaglutide activates the GLP-1 receptor — helping regulate appetite, gastric emptying, and blood sugar. Tirzepatide activates both GLP-1 and GIP receptors, a dual mechanism that clinical trials showed may produce greater average weight reduction for some patients. Your provider will recommend the right starting point.

Are these medications FDA-approved?

Compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are not FDA-approved finished drug products. They are prepared by licensed 503B outsourcing facilities — federally registered compounding pharmacies operating under CGMP standards — and prescribed at the independent clinical judgment of a licensed physician.

Where are the medications made?

All Foundry RX compounds are US-made at 503B-certified outsourcing facilities, batch-tested above 99.9% purity before shipping. We do not source from overseas compounders or unverified pharmacies.

How much weight can I expect to lose?

Clinical trials of brand-name semaglutide showed average weight loss of approximately 15% of body weight over 68 weeks with lifestyle modifications. Tirzepatide trials showed up to 22% average reduction. Individual results vary based on starting weight, adherence, and titration.

What states do you serve?

Foundry RX operates in all 50 states through a network of licensed telehealth providers. Medication ships directly from our certified 503B pharmacy to your door.

Important safety information

GLP-1 receptor agonists including compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide may have serious side effects, including possible thyroid tumors. Do not use if you or your family have a history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) or MEN 2. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved drugs. All medications prescribed at independent clinical judgment of licensed physicians. Common side effects include nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, and constipation.
