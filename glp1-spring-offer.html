Important safety information

GLP-1 receptor agonists including compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide may have serious side effects, including possible thyroid tumors. Do not use if you or your family have a history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) or Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2). These are compounded medications and are not FDA-approved for any specific indication. All medications are prescribed at the independent clinical judgment of US-licensed physicians.
