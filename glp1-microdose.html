More in our full FAQ.

What is GLP-1 microdosing?

A fraction of the typical starting dose, adjusted slowly by your provider, to find the smallest amount that quiets cravings for you.

How much is it?

$99/month on a 3-month plan, billed $297 every 3 months. $119/month month-to-month. Consultation included.

Does the price go up after the first month?

No. On the 3-month plan it's $99 every month.

What happens after I submit?

A licensed provider reviews your answers within 24 hours. They may message you with questions. If treatment is right for you, your prescription ships to your door.

Will I lose muscle?

Your plan pairs a lower dose with a daily protein target and strength training, both of which help protect lean mass.

Is it FDA-approved?

It's compounded in an FDA-registered 503B outsourcing facility in the US. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved or FDA-reviewed for safety or effectiveness.

What about side effects?

The most common are nausea and stomach upset, often when doses increase quickly. Your provider starts low and adjusts slowly. Full safety information.

How is this different from a standard plan?

Standard plans step up toward a full maintenance dose. Microdosing stays at the lowest dose that works for you. See standard options.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. A licensed provider decides if it's right for you.

Important safety information. Prescription only. Do not use with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN2, a prior serious reaction to a GLP-1, or if pregnant or breastfeeding. Common side effects include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, and abdominal pain. Serious risks can include pancreatitis, gallbladder problems, low blood sugar, kidney problems, and mood changes. Seek care for severe stomach pain or signs of allergic reaction.
