More in our full FAQ.

What is GLP-1 microdosing?

A fraction of the typical dose, adjusted slowly, to find the smallest amount that quiets cravings.

How fast will I hear back?

Within 24 hours.

Will I lose muscle?

A lower dose leaves room for protein and training. Your plan is built around both.

How much is it?

$99/month with a 3-month plan, billed $297 every 3 months. $119 month-to-month. Consultation included.

Does the price go up after the first month?

No. With the 3-month plan, it's $99 every month.

Is it FDA-approved?

It's compounded by a licensed US 503B facility. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved or FDA-reviewed for safety or effectiveness.

What about side effects?

Mostly nausea or stomach upset, usually when doses rise fast. Starting low is the point. Full safety info below.

How is this different from a standard plan?

Standard plans climb to a max dose. We stop at the lowest dose that works. See standard options.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. A licensed provider decides if it's right for you.

Important safety information. Prescription only. Do not use with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN2, a prior serious reaction to a GLP-1, or if pregnant or breastfeeding. Common side effects include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, and abdominal pain. Serious risks can include pancreatitis, gallbladder problems, low blood sugar, kidney problems, and mood changes. Seek care for severe stomach pain or signs of allergic reaction.
