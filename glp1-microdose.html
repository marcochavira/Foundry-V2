What is microdosing?

A fraction of the typical dose, adjusted slowly, to find the lowest dose that quiets cravings.

Will I lose muscle?

Lower doses leave room to eat enough protein and keep training, which helps protect lean mass.

How is this different from standard GLP-1 programs?

Standard programs step up toward a maximum dose. We stop at the lowest dose that works. Prefer a standard plan? See our GLP-1 weight loss options.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. A licensed provider reviews your intake and decides if treatment is right for you.

Important safety information. Prescription only. Do not use with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN2, a prior serious reaction to a GLP-1, or if pregnant or breastfeeding. Common side effects include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, and abdominal pain. Serious risks can include pancreatitis, gallbladder problems, low blood sugar, kidney problems, and mood changes. Seek care for severe stomach pain or signs of allergic reaction.

Compounded medications are prepared by a licensed 503B facility and are not FDA-approved. Individual results vary. Not medical advice.
