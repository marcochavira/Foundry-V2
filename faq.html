GLP-1
GLP-1 (glucagon-like peptide-1) receptor agonists are a class of medications that mimic a hormone your body produces naturally after eating. They regulate appetite, slow gastric emptying, and improve insulin sensitivity. The result for most people is reduced hunger, better blood sugar control, and meaningful results over time.
Foundry RX offers physician-supervised semaglutide and tirzepatide protocols, compounded at an FDA-registered 503B facility using USA-made ingredients. Your provider will determine which medication and starting dose is appropriate based on your health history, goals, and candidacy.
Most members notice a reduction in appetite within the first two to four weeks. Measurable changes typically become apparent at weeks four to eight. Meaningful results — typically 10 to 20 percent of body weight — are seen over a three to six month period with consistent adherence to your protocol.
The most common side effects are gastrointestinal — nausea, mild bloating, or changes in bowel habits — particularly in the first few weeks as your body adjusts. These typically resolve as your dose is titrated. Your provider will walk you through what to expect and how to manage any symptoms that arise.
That depends on your goals and how your body responds. Some members use GLP-1 as a short-term intervention, then transition to a maintenance protocol. Others continue long term. Your clinician will guide you through what makes sense for your situation — there is no one-size-fits-all answer.
Appetite regulation and hunger suppression typically return to baseline after discontinuation. Some changes in progress are common without lifestyle interventions in place. Foundry RX providers work with members on exit strategies and maintenance protocols to help sustain results beyond the active treatment period.
Peptides
Peptides are short chains of amino acids — the building blocks of proteins — that act as signaling molecules in the body. They can influence hormone production, cellular repair, immune response, and metabolic function. Foundry RX offers a curated range of research-grade peptide protocols supervised by board-certified clinicians.
