Privacy Policy

Effective May 1, 2026  ·  Last updated May 18, 2026

Algorithm Management Inc, doing business as Foundry RX ("Foundry RX," "we," "us," or "our"), operates foundryrx.co (the "Site"). This page explains our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information we receive from users of the Site and our telehealth services. By using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information Collection & Use

While using our Site, we may ask you to provide personally identifiable information — including your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and health intake information. We use this information only for providing and improving the Site and our clinical services.

Log Data

Like many site operators, we collect information your browser sends when you visit the Site ("Log Data"). This may include your IP address, browser type and version, pages visited, time and date of visit, and other statistics. We may use third-party services such as Google Analytics to collect, monitor, and analyze this data.

Health Information & HIPAA

To the extent that Protected Health Information (PHI) is created or transmitted through our services, we are committed to complying with HIPAA and applicable state privacy laws. Our telehealth platform partner, Uniloop LLC, operates as a Business Associate and maintains HIPAA-compliant infrastructure for the storage and transmission of patient health data.

We will not use or disclose PHI except as permitted by law or with your written authorization. You have the right to access, amend, and request an accounting of disclosures of your health information. Contact us at members@foundryrx.co to exercise these rights.

Communications

We may use your personal information to contact you with membership updates, clinical information, or promotional materials related to your use of Foundry RX. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time.

Cookies

We use cookies to collect information and improve site functionality. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. If you do not accept cookies, some portions of the Site may not function correctly.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Licensed clinicians in our provider network who deliver your care
  • Service providers (telehealth platform, payment processing, pharmacy fulfillment) bound by appropriate data agreements
  • Legal or regulatory authorities as required by law, including LegitScript certification compliance obligations

Jurisdictions Served

Foundry RX provides telehealth services to patients in all 50 U.S. states and Washington D.C. Services are not available outside the United States. Provider availability may vary by state.

Security

The security of your personal information is important to us. We implement commercially reasonable safeguards to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy is effective as of May 1, 2026 and will remain in effect except with respect to any changes in its provisions, which will be in effect immediately after being posted on this page. We reserve the right to update this policy at any time. Your continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes your acknowledgment and consent to the modified policy. If we make material changes, we will notify you via email or by placing a prominent notice on the Site.
